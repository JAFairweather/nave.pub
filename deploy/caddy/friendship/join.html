Get Amperfy

It's a free music player for iPhone. In the App Store, search for Amperfy and install it.

The project's page is at github.com/BLeeEZ/amperfy.

3

Start with the best-sounding tapes

In Amperfy, go to Library → Playlists. Look for Slightly Stoopid - Best sound.

Every song in it comes from the cleanest recording of the shows James has. The recordings were ranked by sound quality by signal processing algorithms. More bands will get their own Best sound playlist soon.

Want a whole night instead? Open Albums. Each show is listed as its date and venue, for example 1977-05-08 - Barton Hall, Cornell University.
